# Changelog

All notable changes to Relay are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Added `AGENTS.md` with guidance for AI coding assistants.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-03

- Initial commercial release.
- Photo-led homepage with an autoplaying featured slideshow, trending and category rails, topic tiles, and a latest-posts section that runs a card grid over editorial rows beside a sidebar of editor's picks, most-read stories, and contributors.
- Article pages with a title-first header, an 820px reading column, a reading-progress bar, share and copy-link actions, an author box, and a related-stories rail.
- Read time calculated from the article body at build time at a configurable reading speed, so no post carries it in frontmatter.
- Category, tag, author, archive, search, about, contact, privacy, and 404 pages.
- Configurable per-category archive layouts: featured, grid, and editorial list.
- Client-side search with a keyboard-friendly command palette and a dedicated results page.
- Markdown content collections with validated frontmatter.
- Monochrome design system with a single signal accent, self-hosted Geist, and system-aware light and dark modes.
- Scroll-snap rails that page horizontally on small screens and collapse into a grid on large ones, with no carousel dependency.
- Responsive Astro images, canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Accessible skip link, landmarks, focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Provider-neutral newsletter and contact form configuration.
